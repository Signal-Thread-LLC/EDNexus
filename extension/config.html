EDNexus commander card

Shows your live Elite Dangerous session to viewers as a card they can pull out over the stream and hide again.

Backend service

Leave this alone unless you run your own EBS. It must match the EbsBaseUrl set in the desktop app under Settings → Twitch.

What viewers see

Choose this in the desktop app: Settings → Twitch → Stream card. Each section — commander, credits, ship, location, fleet carrier, exobiology, mining, missions, cargo — is opt-in, and anything switched off is never sent off your machine at all.
